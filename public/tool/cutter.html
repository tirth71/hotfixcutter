Operator quick workflow

Follow these steps for each production batch. Confirm the physical sheet yourself; this page is not connected to machine sensors.

1. Select design
Choose a saved design or upload a new one.
2. Set quantity
Choose a preset or enter a custom target.
3. Load & align sheet
Place one sheet manually and check its position.
4. Review cut preview
Check every boundary; do not cut through stones or motifs.
5. Confirm collection
Check the cut pieces and empty the tray when needed.
6. Complete sheet
Update the counter only after one sheet is physically finished.

Full workflow

  1. Set sheet size13 in or 16 in wide, 30 in long, 3 mm rectangle margin
  2. Upload JPG/PNGFix orientation, crop the catalogue header
  3. Pick Butta or Patta modeSet direction, strip length, thickness, bridge gap
  4. DetectCheck mask and candidate count
  5. Review rectanglesAdd, delete, or edit X/Y/Width/Height
  6. CalibrateFull-sheet image gives pixels to millimetres
  7. Export SVGRectangle paths only, no stone outlines
  8. Convert to G-codeCAM step: origin, travel limits, feed, blade offset
  9. Pen dry runCheck origin, orientation, travel, collisions
  10. Blade cutGuard on, hand clear, E-stop within reach

Simulation only. Real jobs need a validated SVG-to-G-code stage and a first pen run after any calibration or mechanical change.
